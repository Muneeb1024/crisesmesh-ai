CrisesMesh AI — Government Multi-Crises Coordination Architecture
Core Vision

CrisesMesh AI is a mobile-first Government Authority Agentic AI Coordination System designed to manage multiple simultaneous crises in real time.

The system continuously monitors signals, detects incidents, predicts severity, prioritizes emergencies, allocates limited resources, coordinates stakeholders, simulates actions, and supports human authorities with AI-driven operational intelligence.

The platform is not a simple reporting app.
It is an AI-powered emergency operations ecosystem.

System Approach
Single App — Multiple Roles

For the prototype and hackathon phase, the system will use one mobile application with role-based access.

Role 1 — Citizen

Citizen side focuses on:

reporting emergencies
requesting help
receiving alerts
viewing safe routes
checking nearby crisis zones

Citizens act as one of the live signal sources of the system.

Role 2 — Government Authority

Government side acts as the AI-powered command center.

Authorities can:

monitor all active crises
supervise AI agents
approve critical actions
view live operational intelligence
allocate resources
manage emergency coordination

The Government Command Center is not accessible to citizens.

Government Dashboard Architecture
Home Dashboard Purpose

The Government Dashboard should provide:

Real-time operational awareness of all active crises.

The dashboard is not focused on a single incident.

It acts as a national/city-level AI coordination overview.

1. Situation Overview

Top-level operational summary showing:

active crises
critical incidents
resource utilization

Instant awareness for authorities.

Example:

Heat Emergency — High

Each crisis card shows:

crisis type
location
severity

Multi-Agent Orchestration Workflow

The system operates continuously as a 24/7 AI orchestration layer.

Authorities do not manually run the system.

The AI agents continuously monitor and coordinate operations.
