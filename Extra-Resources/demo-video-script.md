# CrisesMesh AI — 3-5 Minute Demo Video Script

**Title**: CrisesMesh AI — Intelligent Multi-Crisis Coordination  
**Duration**: ~4.5 Minutes  
**Narration Style**: Clear, professional English narration with Roman Urdu crisis notification examples.  
**Cast/Roles**: Narrator / Command Coordinator.

---

## Act 1 — The Emergency Report (0:00 - 1:00)

### Scene 1: The Monsoon Crisis
* **Visual**: Show the mobile landing page at `http://localhost:8081` on screen.
* **UI Action**: Click **"Continue as Citizen"**.
* **Audio/Narration**: 
  > *"Every year, Pakistan’s monsoon season causes widespread damage due to urban flooding. Fragmented telemetry and slow emergency pipelines delay critical response times. Enter CrisesMesh AI, a dual-role crisis platform designed to bridge the gap between citizens and emergency command."*

### Scene 2: Submitting the Signal
* **Visual**: The Citizen Onboarding page is shown. Complete onboarding with a mock name (*Ahmad*) and phone number. Proceed to the Citizen Home screen.
* **UI Action**: Click **"Report Urban Flooding"**, select **"Urban Flooding"** as Category and **"Severe"** as Severity.
* **UI Action**: Type: *"G-10 Underpass is flooded, water levels are rising rapidly! Drain blocked."* into the description field.
* **UI Action**: Click **"Submit Report"**. Show the beautiful green submission success modal.
* **Audio/Narration**: 
  > *"As a citizen, Ahmad notices severe flooding at the G-10 Underpass in Islamabad. Through the clean Citizen interface, he submits a localized report. The app captures his location and instantly transmits the complaint as a critical threat signal to the backend."*

---

## Act 2 — The Command Center (1:00 - 2:30)

### Scene 3: Accessing Command Room
* **Visual**: Go back to the landing screen, select **"Government Command Center"**.
* **UI Action**: Type the secure coordinator PIN `1122` and click **"Access Command Center"**.
* **Audio/Narration**: 
  > *"On the other side, emergency dispatchers monitor the twin cities from a dark command-center deck. Logged in with command PIN 1122, we see a dashboard displaying active telemetry, simulation states, and resource indicators."*

### Scene 4: Multi-Signal Fusion
* **Visual**: The Government Home screen displays one active incident card at the G-10 Underpass.
* **UI Action**: Click on the active incident card to launch the **Incident Details & AI Fusion** screen.
* **Audio/Narration**: 
  > *"The coordinator opens the active crisis card. Here, our AI Signal Fusion Agent merges 7 distinct signals—citizen reports, weather updates, traffic congestion, water-sensors, and field feeds. The Classification and Severity agents categorize this as a Critical Urban Flooding event with a priority index of 95/100, calculating a threat radius of 1.2 kilometers."*

### Scene 5: Dispatched Units & Rerouting
* **Visual**: Scroll down to the resource panel. Highlight the AI resource recommendation.
* **UI Action**: Click **"Approve Resource Allocation"** and then select **"Reroute Sim"** tab to display the interactive Leaflet map routing overlay.
* **Audio/Narration**: 
  > *"Based on severity, the Resource Allocation Agent suggests deploying 4 rescue teams, 2 ambulances, and 4 water pumps. We approve the allocation instantly. Next, we simulate transit routes. The Simulation Agent computes unsafe routes passing through the flood zone compared to a safer, optimal route detour, calculating arrival times and traffic impacts to protect our first responders."*

---

## Act 3 — Public Broadcast (2:30 - 3:30)

### Scene 6: AI-Drafted Broadcasts
* **Visual**: Navigate to the Alert Approval panel.
* **UI Action**: Click **"Approve Public Alert"** to open the draft editor.
* **UI Action**: Click **"Generate AI Alert Draft"** to populate the bilingual drafts.
* **UI Action**: Highlight the English and Roman Urdu texts:
  * *EN: "Avoid G-10 underpass. Urban flooding risk detected."*
  * *UR: "G-10 underpass se parhez karein. Pani bharne ka khatra detect hua hai."*
* **UI Action**: Enable in-app, SMS, and WhatsApp checkboxes, and click **"Approve & Publish"**.
* **Audio/Narration**: 
  > *"To safeguard the public, we broadcast localized warnings. Our Notification Agent drafts bilingual warnings in English and Roman Urdu. We review the draft, authorize SMS and WhatsApp simulations, and push it live."*

### Scene 7: Citizen Alert Warning
* **Visual**: Switch back to the **Citizen Module**, open the **"Live Alert Demo"** page.
* **UI Action**: Show the active red banner warning card and click **"Play Warning UR"** and **"Play Warning EN"** (mimic audio warning). Dismiss the alert.
* **Audio/Narration**: 
  > *"Instantly, citizens in the high-risk G-10 zone receive the warning. The app triggers a loud siren along with localized Urdu TTS voice warnings: 'Warning: Red Zone qareeb hai. Is raaste se parhez karein.' This prevents traffic from entering the flooded underpass."*

---

## Act 4 — False Alarm Recovery & Submission (3:30 - 4:30)

### Scene 8: The Recovery Loop
* **Visual**: Return to the Government Command Center dashboard.
* **UI Action**: Scroll to the recovery module, and click **"Trigger Verification Conflict"**.
* **UI Action**: Show the updated incident card reclassified to **"Water-Main Burst"** and showing the public flood alert status as **"Retracted / Corrected"**.
* **Audio/Narration**: 
  > *"But what if the telemetry was incorrect? A field officer inspects the underpass and reports that the flooding is actually due to a localized water-main burst, not regional rain accumulation. The coordinator triggers a verification conflict. The Recovery Agent reclassifies the incident, updates dispatched assets, retracts the public flood broadcast, and automatically notifies utility repair teams. Our command loop remains completely self-correcting."*

### Scene 9: Antigravity Trace Evidence
* **Visual**: Open the VS Code workspace, highlighting the `antigravity-evidence/` folder.
* **UI Action**: Expand `traces/`, `test-logs/`, `screenshots/`, and `demo-recordings/`.
* **Audio/Narration**: 
  > *"Every single agent action, test log, and planning cycle is archived inside our 'antigravity-evidence' folder, proving full validation and traceability throughout our development lifecycle. CrisesMesh AI proves that Pakistani cities can transition to unified, proactive crisis coordination. Thank you."*

* **Visual**: Fade out with the project name and credentials.
